About the cabin

Plenty of space, great views and lots of living areas

Welcome to a large and inviting cabin built in 2000, approx. 130 m² (+ a loft of approx. 30 m²), in an attractive location with lovely views over the mountains. The cabin has a well-planned layout with plenty of room for family and guests.

You get no fewer than 7 bedrooms, 4 of them on a practical loft, plus a garage.

Lounge with pool table

Evenings by the fire and the pool table

The lounge with the pool table and TV corner has its own wood stove, a large sofa and stairs up to the loft. The dining room has a long table, a wood stove and its own TV corner.

This cabin combines plenty of space, great views and lots of living areas. Ideal for active weekends and holidays all year round.

Summer and winter

A place for every season

Location

Panoramic views over Gudbrandsdalen

Hafjell Panorama offers a spectacular location with panoramic views over the Gudbrandsdalen valley and plenty of sun. You stay in the middle of a year-round paradise, close to skiing, golf and wonderful nature. Hafjell Alpine Centre, one of Norway's best ski resorts, is close by, and excellent cross-country trails start right outside the door.

In summer the area opens up for cycling in varied terrain, lovely trails and outdoor experiences for the whole family. Hafjell Golf is one of the most scenic golf courses in Norway, and beautiful mountains for summit hikes are a short trip away.
